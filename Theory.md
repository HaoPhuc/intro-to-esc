**Motivation for the project**

The power output *P* of a tokamak is:
**P ~ n_T * n_D * T**
where:
n_T : density of tritium in the core
n_D : density of deuterium in the core
T : temperature in the core

# Q: Why do we have to use 2 gases instead of 1?

Greenwald density limit *n_GW* is a theoretical hard limit on total density:
**n_GW > n_D + n_T**

For constant *T*, maximum *P* is achieve when *n_T * n_D* is maximum. We know that n_T * n_D = 0.5 * [(n_T + n_D)**2 - (n_T**2 + n_D**2)]
